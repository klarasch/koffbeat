## Soft mallet tap sound and optional melody

- Branch: `wip/mallet-melody` (commit c9f6b10)
- What it does: replaces the felt piano tap with a short soft-mallet note that has a clear
  start and end, adds an optional melody per level (D major pentatonic, to fit the drone; the
  left hand plays an octave lower) with a "Melody" switch on home, and a muted thud for
  stray taps.
- Open questions: piano or mallet? The thud on stray taps ("pft") may feel off.
